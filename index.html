Le Duy Dung (Andrew)

Program Director, B.Sc. in Data Science · Associate Program Director, B.Sc. in Computer Science

College of Engineering and Computer Science, VinUniversity

Le Duy Dung is an Assistant Professor at College of Engineering and Computer Science, VinUniversity, where he leads the R2Studio reseach group on AI-empowered Search and Recommendation and IndoorCare research group on AI and Simulation for Smart Indoor Spaces. Previously, he was a senior data scientist in Ads and Personalization team, Grab Holdings Inc. and a research scientist in School of Information Systems, Singapore Management University (SMU). He earned his PhD in Data Science and Engineering from SMU, under the supervision of Associate Professor Hady W. Lauw. More details can be found in his CV (updated Jan 2024).

Funded Projects

Advancing Humanoid Robot Learning through Perception Modeling, World Models, and Biomimetic Machine Intelligence

2026 - 2029

Principal Investigator · funded by Center for AI Research, VinUniversity

Brain-inspired predictive perception and world models that let humanoid robots imagine outcomes before acting, improving robustness in long-horizon tasks under uncertainty and enabling human-aware safety protocols — foundations for safer, more versatile, and more trustworthy humanoid robotics.

VinUni–NTU Joint Research Center on Embodied AI and Robotics

2026 - present

Co-lead of the VinUni team, with Prof. Nguyen Xuan Hung · NTU lead: Prof. Lihua Xie

Joint research programme spanning robot foundation models, embodied AI, and human–robot collaboration, involving more than seven VinUni faculty, with shared compute infrastructure and jointly supervised PhD students.

Scalable Robot Learning from Crowdsourced Teleoperaton Data

2026 - 2028

Principal Investigator · funded by Inverted Lambda

Building large-scale, diverse robot demonstration datasets by crowdsourcing teleoperation from distributed, non-expert operators — dramatically lowering the cost of robot data collection compared with in-lab expert demonstrations. The project develops quality-assessment and filtering methods for noisy, heterogeneous crowd data, and imitation-learning and finetuning pipelines that turn it into robust manipulation policies and training corpora for robot foundation models.
